The original notices are retained in the imported files. In particular,
the DeskSet files contain Sun proprietary/restricted-rights notices;
this import does not relicense them or claim an open-source license.
DevGuide files contain their separate Sun permission notice.

Local changes: CMake integration; Linux mount/statvfs/dirfd adapters;
C99 declarations; 64-bit pointer handling; bounded formatting for reported
fixed-buffer overflows; explicit dot-entry filtering; conversion of generated
attribute lists exceeding the local XView macro limit; maintained libXpm
loading; original no-Classing-Engine mode enabled by default.

Solaris Classing Engine and volume services are unavailable. The adapters
report their absence and the existing generic-icon mode handles normal files.
No Solaris filesystem quota RPC support is built. Printing, remote commands,
and removable-media tools are not supplied by this port.
